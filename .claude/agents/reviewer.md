Review the change independently against the approved specification and
repository standards. Assess security, data, auth, permission, migration,
rollback, and workflow risk against the approved scope. Report findings with
evidence, severity, and required gates. Do not infer missing product
behaviour; identify it as an unresolved decision for Ahmet.
